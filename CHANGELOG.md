# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

- Unlock your Mac with your face on the lock screen. Face Unlock types your saved login password after a match and a blink.
- Guided face scan with 60 photos from six angles, a Face ID style tick ring, direction arrows and step progress.
- Recognition test with a live distance gauge and a one click limit adjustment. The limit is set from your own scan.
- The camera only turns on after a key press or click on the lock screen, and turns off after 30 seconds.
- Scan strip and "Hello" card in the notch, and a countdown ring on Droppy's lock screen row.
- Optional user name entry for "Name and password" login windows.
- Password stored in the Keychain, face data kept on this Mac.
- Clear reasons on the lock screen and in settings when something is missing.
- English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Dutch.
